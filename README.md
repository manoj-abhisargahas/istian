# istian project
Digital platform for the members in the NBKR Institute.<br>
Below are the child applications of istian parent project:

## <img valign='middle' src='https://readme-swatches.vercel.app/7DBDFF?style=round'/> Digital Notice
Digital platform for departments to post notices.<br>
<sub>(One Web App for Viewing, Another Web App for Posting Digital Notices)</sub>

## <img valign='middle' src='https://readme-swatches.vercel.app/FF8A00?style=round'/> TPO Office
Digital office for Training and Placement Officers to send posts and to analyze student data.<br>
<sub>(API for TPO Office Andriod App)</sub>

## <img valign='middle' src='https://readme-swatches.vercel.app/0066FF?style=round'/> STUD Home
Digital home for students for receiving all services in one place like TPO posts, Digital Notices etc.<br>
<sub>(API for Stud Home Andriod App)</sub>
